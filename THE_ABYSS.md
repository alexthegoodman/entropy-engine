# The Abyss

## Story

In The Abyss (FPS RPG), an energy-obsessed, highly-advanced surface people drill deep into their planet in search of more energy. They accidentally breach and destroy a large pocket of magical energy which has been the source of religion and magical power to a hidden, underground civilization for millennia. War ensues for 1000 years. There’s a brief ceasefire early on the war because both sides realized that mutual annihilation was inevitable. After the ceasefire, they kept on fighting even as civilization on both sides struggled. This first game in the series starts 224 years into the war. The first chapter of this game begins at the legendary Meeting Place, where the ceasefire was ratified nearly 200 years previous. Now it is in ruins, and overrun with hostiles. There is however a weapon cache inside the large Meeting Dome, which includes surface weapons and magical ones. The player can use both weapon types.

The Meeting Place was a place of commerce and community between the two sides until about 100 years into the war, as they needed things from each other despite the ongoing war. After the Meeting Dome was deserted, civilization finally deteriorated into the apocalyptic scenes because neither side had access to vital resources.

The first character you play as is a rogue. Rogues do as they please, but they’re known as looters. He was raised by a surface mechanic who had a child with an underground mage. A very rare and dangerous thing to have happen, but sometimes it does. He is raised by his father on the surface. Then he eventually ventures off to the underground to understand his past. The story starts some years after that, at the Meeting Place. You begin lightly armed and stocked, but need to find more items and weapons soon, because there are so many hostiles everywhere.

As a rogue, you have allegiance to neither side. Regularly, factions from each side get into battles in the game world. It’s up to you whether you get involved, and which side you pretend to be on. Because if you attack someone’s enemy, then they will befriend you. You can also befriend hostiles. It’s a choice between looting their bodies so you can become powerful, or earning friends so you can achieve a renown victory in the game. There’s a story, but you can choose to accept a story victory or to keep playing for renown or power victory.

The Abyss includes, of course, sword and gun combat, primarily, as well as magic. The magic comes down from the heavens or from the ground, usually, rather than being projectile. It also includes telekinesis and mind control (Full NPC control). The technology the surface people have will including lightning gun and xray vision goggles.

Only rogues travel alone, everyone else travels in groups. Most groups have an official military affiliation and are actively engaged in the war, but resources are spread thin. It’s also important to note that each side has degraded into many factions, each loyal to one side, but not always staying in close coordination with each other. There is one faction on each side for each region of the open world, so you gain Favor Points towards one of the factions of that particular region.

I like the idea of hostiles being highlighted red, and then you can tag a group as green by tapping a button. No need to pull out the satellite device because this is just to protect friendly fire. Either way, they won't shoot at you unless you shoot at them first, or if they give you a warning and you do need heed it. This way, the social dynamics are easily enjoyed, regardless of your chosen victory path.

## Chapter 1 

Main Quest: Get inside the Meeting Dome and Loot It

- Finish using your satellite device and look up to the apocalyptic world around you, this introduces the mini-map and indicates where the Dome is relative to your location
- Kill a small, evil worm monster which are sometimes found near water, this introduces the basic controls
- Begin on your journey, it is wise to scavenge surrounding abandoned buildings for resources
- Make your way closer to the dome, and you are forced into the middle of skirmish between two factions, and the tagging tutorial is completed by the player so they understand that mechanic
    - Player makes their choice: kill all or kill one side, but if you wait too long they’ll all turn on you
    - A Suspicion Meter can help indicate when people are about to attack, while an Eye icon next to that meter can indicate whether you are visible to anybody currently
    - Music shifts from eerie to action
- After that situation is diffused, you make your way into the dome where plenty of historical artifacts remain which explain the background of the story
- Right before you can get the weapon cache, maybe someone from the opposing side knocks you out and drags you away, which leads into Chapter 2 and also concludes the first demo.

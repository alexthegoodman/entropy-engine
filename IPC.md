# Entropy IPC

Integrate your app with other apps. Render using other software's graphical rendering pipelines (via serializable config data), leverage their functions and capabilities within your own app, and expose other useful things for interop purposes.

If you expose 80% of your apps capabilities via Entropy IPC, you qualify for the Entropy marketplace, and enjoy free promotion in the marketplace.

This allows people to build apps the combine capabilities from other apps or create innovative integrations.

We need a centralized app for permissions handling.

Apps will have auto-docs that describe what they expose.

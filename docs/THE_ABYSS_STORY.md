and human intelligence in the government's ranks, including parliament. Although nearly all humans were cyborgs. Robots live alongside humans. They have exceeded their own energy use, and use inter-planetary methods

Narrative Design Document — Story Overview

## Story Premise

The Abyss is a science-fantasy action FPS-RPG set on a planet inhabited by two civilizations that have developed independently for thousands of years.

On the surface, humanity has achieved a technologically advanced, unified civilization governed jointly by humans and superintelligent artificial intelligence. Beneath the planet's crust, numerous independent kingdoms have flourished through the use of magic derived from a sacred river and its mysterious source of energy.

When surface scientists accidentally destroy the source while investigating an unknown radiation signature, millions perish. The underground kingdoms interpret the catastrophe as a deliberate attack against their civilizations and their religion. Their retaliation provokes a devastating military response from the surface.

What begins as first contact rapidly escalates into a planetary war. The once-unified surface civilization fractures into competing nations, ancient underground kingdoms mobilize their armies, and entire cities are reduced to ruins.

The player follows Alex, a surface military officer who participates in the initial expedition into the Abyss. Over the course of the conflict, Alex loses faith in the institutions he once served and eventually joins a rogue faction composed of people from both civilizations.

Although the faction seeks to end the war, its members must fight to survive. Alex forms close relationships with underground inhabitants, develops a bond with a flying creature native to the Abyss, and ultimately helps negotiate a fragile peace.

The treaty fails, and Alex is executed.

His death marks neither the end of the conflict nor the destruction of his ideals. The war will continue for another thousand years.

## Narrative Structure

ACT I

Chapters 1–3

## The Descent

First contact, religious catastrophe, and the outbreak of war

### Chapter 1: First Blood

Alex arrives at the site of the drilling catastrophe as part of the first military expedition into the Abyss. The enormous fracture reveals an underground world far larger and more developed than surface scientists anticipated. As the expedition descends, it encounters hostile underground warriors who believe the surface civilization has deliberately destroyed their sacred source of magic. The resulting battle devastates the expedition and establishes immediate hostility between the two worlds. Alex survives, but the encounter leaves both sides convinced that the other represents a dangerous enemy.

### Chapter 2: Retaliation

The surface government responds to the attack by deploying overwhelming military force into the Abyss. Alex participates in operations intended to secure the fracture, rescue survivors, and establish permanent military positions. Surface forces initially enjoy a technological advantage, but underground armies demonstrate magical capabilities that challenge even the most advanced weapons. The fighting spreads into populated settlements, producing enormous civilian casualties. Meanwhile, underground kingdoms begin coordinating their military responses, transforming the catastrophe into a religious and territorial war.

### Chapter 3: The Sacred Dead

During an operation deep within the Abyss, Alex becomes separated from his unit and encounters survivors of a devastated underground settlement. Among them is a religious scholar or healer who reluctantly assists him. Through this encounter, Alex learns that the destroyed energy source was not merely the foundation of underground magic but the center of its religious traditions. He also encounters an underground warrior whose family was killed in the catastrophe, establishing a bitter personal rivalry. Although Alex returns to his military responsibilities, he begins to understand that the war originated from a catastrophic misunderstanding rather than deliberate aggression.

ACT II

Chapters 4–6

## The Fracturing

Escalation, collapsing political unity, and the destruction of familiar worlds

### Chapter 4: The Beast Below

As the war expands, Alex is assigned to operations near a surviving underground city during a temporary ceasefire. The player witnesses the beauty and complexity of underground civilization, including its architecture, markets, religious institutions, and magical environment. Alex also discovers an injured flying creature native to the Abyss and chooses to protect it, beginning a lasting bond. Meanwhile, political disagreements emerge within the surface government over the conduct of the war. Some leaders demand total victory, while others advocate negotiation or withdrawal. The first serious cracks appear in the surface civilization's longstanding unity.

### Chapter 5: The Burning Kingdom

A major attack breaks the ceasefire, and surface forces launch a devastating offensive against the underground city Alex previously visited. Alex participates in the fighting but disobeys orders to prevent the deaths of civilians, including individuals he has come to know. The city is largely destroyed, demonstrating how quickly the war can erase centuries of cultural achievement. At the same time, underground armies launch increasingly destructive retaliatory attacks against surface population centers. Political tensions reach a breaking point as regional governments and military leaders openly challenge the unified surface administration.

### Chapter 6: The Shattered Union

The surface civilization formally fractures into competing nations following political upheaval, military mutinies, and disputes over control of the Abyss. Some successor states pursue aggressive expansion, others seek peace, and several turn their weapons against former allies. Alex returns to the surface and witnesses the destruction of cities once considered secure and prosperous. Soldiers who previously fought together now serve rival governments. As the war becomes increasingly fragmented, Alex struggles to determine where his loyalties belong. His growing refusal to participate in indiscriminate violence places him in direct conflict with his superiors.

ACT III

Chapters 7–8

## The Exiles

Disillusionment, unlikely alliances, and the creation of a third power

### Chapter 7: Broken Allegiance

Alex is ordered to participate in an operation against a settlement suspected of assisting an enemy surface nation and harboring underground refugees. When the mission threatens to become a massacre, he refuses his orders and turns against members of his own military command. Branded a traitor, Alex is forced to flee into the Abyss. He is eventually reunited with the underground scholar and the warrior he previously fought, both of whom have become disillusioned with their own leaders. Their uneasy cooperation develops into mutual respect as they survive attacks from multiple factions. Alex's growing flying companion becomes essential to their escape and movement through the underground world.

### Chapter 8: No Nation

Alex and his companions join a growing rogue faction composed of surface deserters, underground warriors, refugees, and independent robots. The faction seeks to protect civilians and establish cooperation between the civilizations but maintains a capable fighting force to defend its territory. Alex becomes one of its principal warriors and military leaders, participating in operations against hostile forces from both worlds. The faction uncovers compelling evidence that the original drilling catastrophe was accidental and begins attempting to bring this information to the competing governments. Meanwhile, Alex forms deep personal attachments to his new companions and finds a sense of belonging outside the nations that have consumed themselves through war.

ACT IV

Chapters 9–10

## The Last Accord

A fragile peace, betrayal, and the beginning of a thousand-year tragedy

### Chapter 9: The Last Accord

After years of devastating conflict, the rogue faction succeeds in bringing representatives from several surface nations and underground kingdoms into negotiations. Alex and his companions undertake dangerous missions to protect diplomats, secure trade routes, exchange prisoners, and prevent extremist factions from sabotaging the process. His flying companion now allows him to travel freely between the Abyss and the surface, symbolizing his connection to both worlds. The negotiations culminate in a historic treaty establishing limited peace, humanitarian cooperation, and recognition between participating nations. For a brief period, Alex believes the war might finally be brought under control, and the faction's refuge becomes a symbol of coexistence.

### Chapter 10: The Price of Peace

The treaty collapses following a catastrophic attack that reignites hostilities between the participating nations. Political leaders blame the rogue faction, accusing its members of manipulating the negotiations and conspiring against their governments. The faction's refuge is assaulted, and Alex leads a desperate defense to protect his companions and the civilian population. Despite his efforts, the settlement falls, and Alex is captured. Facing the threatened execution of his surviving allies, he agrees to surrender in exchange for their release. He is publicly executed as a traitor, while his companions escape into the Abyss carrying the evidence of the original catastrophe. The war resumes on an even greater scale, and the possibility of lasting peace disappears for generations.

## Epilogue: The Thousand-Year War

Alex's execution becomes one of the defining events of the conflict's earliest years. His surviving companions scatter across the planet, preserving the history of the catastrophe and the ideals of the rogue faction.

Over the following centuries, the surface nations continue to divide, consolidate, and wage war. Underground kingdoms rise and fall, magical traditions evolve, and entire civilizations are transformed by the prolonged conflict.

Alex's legacy becomes contested. Some remember him as a traitor, others as a ruthless soldier, and a few as one of the first people to recognize the possibility of coexistence.

The game concludes by revealing that the conflict will continue for a thousand years, establishing the foundation for future stories set throughout the history of the Abyss.

## Gameplay and Narrative Priorities

The game should emphasize the contrast between exploration and destruction. Cities, settlements, and landscapes should change as the war progresses, allowing players to revisit familiar locations and witness the consequences of military campaigns.

Combat remains a central pillar throughout the story. Alex begins as a technologically advanced surface soldier and gradually learns to fight alongside underground warriors and magic users. His evolving relationships create opportunities for companion-based gameplay, while the flying creature provides new traversal and exploration possibilities as it matures.

The political fragmentation of the surface should also be reflected in gameplay. Formerly friendly military installations may become hostile, rival nations may offer competing missions, and the player's alliances should influence access to territories and resources.

The rogue faction provides the central hub for the later game, offering a place for companion interactions, faction development, military preparation, and limited trade between civilizations.

The ending is intentionally tragic and predetermined: Alex cannot prevent his own execution or end the war. However, player decisions can determine which companions survive, which settlements are protected, and what remains of the rogue faction after his death.

Ultimately, The Abyss is not a story about a hero who saves the world. It is the story of a warrior who discovers a cause worth fighting for, builds a family among his former enemies, and dies attempting to preserve a peace that the world is not yet ready to accept.

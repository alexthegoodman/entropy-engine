# Future Plans

Even though the addon ecosystem is insanely important, so is photorealism and visual fidelity:

Volumetric effects are huge - volumetric fog, god rays, and atmospheric scattering that interact with light sources. Think sunbeams cutting through a forest canopy or fog rolling through valleys.
Advanced lighting systems beyond what you already have - global illumination that bounces light realistically, real-time ray tracing for reflections and shadows, and dynamic time-of-day systems where everything responds naturally as the sun moves across the sky.
Particle systems that feel alive - embers floating from fires, dust motes in shafts of light, realistic smoke and steam, magical effects. The key is having them respond to wind, gravity, and collisions properly.
Material and surface detail through physically-based rendering (PBR). Surfaces that look genuinely metallic, wet, rough, or smooth. Subsurface scattering for things like skin, wax, or leaves where light penetrates slightly. Parallax occlusion mapping to give flat textures convincing depth.
Vegetation systems with wind animation that looks organic rather than uniform. Trees and grass that sway with different frequencies, foliage that rustles convincingly.
Weather systems - dynamic rain with proper surface wetness, snow accumulation, storms with lightning that illuminates the scene.
Water variations - you've nailed ocean water, but also consider rivers with flow maps, waterfalls with mist, puddles with proper reflections, caustics dancing on underwater surfaces.
